How it works

  1. You send your menu and details. A photo, PDF, or paper list is fine.
  2. Fixed price is agreed before work starts. No work starts until the price and scope are agreed in writing.
  3. Draft page is shared for 1 revision round. You check the menu, prices, hours, and location.
  4. Final page is handed over. You also get a short note on how to change a price later.

Payment terms are agreed before work starts. For direct business clients, payment is by Payoneer Request a Payment, or through Fiverr if you prefer a marketplace. No Stripe, and no PayPal receive, is offered.
